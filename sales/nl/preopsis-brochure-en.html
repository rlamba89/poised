The challenge

An incomplete screening costs a slot in the theatre schedule.

!

Pressure on the pre-op clinic

Fewer nurses and anaesthetists for the same number of operations, or more.

!

Waiting lists

Every theatre slot counts. A screening that is incomplete at the last minute means a moved or empty slot.

!

Re-typing and chasing

Answers are copied into the EHR and missing details are chased by phone.

!

Language and literacy

Not every patient reads Dutch easily. A questionnaire has to be clear to everyone.

How it works

One route, from request to recovery

1

Invitation from the EHR

As soon as the operation is requested, the patient receives an SMS and email with a personal link.

2

Questionnaire at home

On their own phone, with no app or password: open the link, enter their date of birth and start. Answers save automatically and reminders follow on their own.

3

Automatic triage

From the answers, PreOpsis suggests an ASA grade and risk scores. Low-risk patients with no flags move to ‘Ready for admission’. Others get a phone or clinic consultation. Every outcome is visible and can be reversed.

ASA I–IICompleted digitally
ASA III–IVConsultation
Rules set by each hospital
4

Validation by the pre-op team

The nurse or anaesthetist reviews each section, corrects where needed and always sees what the patient answered.

5

Pre-op summary back in the EHR

The summary with coded findings (SNOMED CT, ICD-10) is ready for the anaesthetist and the theatre team.

6

Recovery at home

After discharge the patient answers recovery questions and PROMs on the same phone. Your team sees who is falling behind.

PreOpsis works alongside your EHR

The EHR stays the source of truth. PreOpsis takes the patient and the request, handles contact with the patient and returns the result.

Pre-op team and anaesthetist

  • Validation per section, with the patient's own answers alongside
  • Suggested ASA grade and risk scores such as STOP-Bang and Apfel
  • Findings with codes, notes and amber and red flags
  • Worklists: ready for review, not responding, consultation needed
  • Pre-op summary on screen and as a PDF

The patient

  • Works on any phone, with no app or password
  • One question per screen, saved automatically, pick up later
  • SMS and email reminders on day 2 and day 5
  • In Dutch, English, Turkish, Arabic, Polish and other languages
  • For a next operation, earlier answers are already filled in

Your questionnaires, your rules

  • Manage questions, logic and scores yourself, without programming
  • Test cases check after every change that outcomes are still right
  • Published versions are locked; a new version takes one click
  • For the whole organisation or per location
  • Translations side by side, with missing text highlighted

After surgery

  • Recovery check at home, for example at 7 days and 6 weeks
  • PROMs with a baseline: EQ-5D-5L, Oxford Hip and Knee Score, HOOS-PS and KOOS-PS
  • Measurement points aligned with the LROI (Dutch arthroplasty register)
  • A task for the team when a score drops
  • Reporting per procedure
