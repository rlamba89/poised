# S20 answers

Asked by the conductor (`/run-sessions S18 S22`), 20 Oct 2026.

1. **The transition table:** **accepted as in the brief** (recommended).
2. **Correcting a mistake:** Rahul: "one or many steps back - they can undo, but one step at a time". Follow-up answer: **any clinician can undo any manual step.** Each undo goes one step back, to the status before the latest move, with a reason, and can be repeated to go further back. (The conductor's recommendation was a super clinician only; Rahul chose any clinician.) Cancel stays final (answer 3).
3. **Cancel reasons:** **yes** (recommended). A fixed list: procedure cancelled, patient withdrew, moved to another hospital, other. Other needs a sentence. A cancelled episode can't be un-cancelled, only archived (S22).
4. **When is a reason required?** **For cancel, re-open and a step back (undo)** (recommended). It's optional for other manual moves.
5. **What cancelling does to the patient:** **yes** (recommended). Their invite link and sessions for the episode end, follow-up jobs stop, and their home shows "Cancelled by the hospital" with no form.
6. **Re-open:** **yes** (recommended). It clears the review stamp and unlocks the clinician's answers. Answers and validations stay, the episode goes back to Ready for review, and the patient isn't asked again.
