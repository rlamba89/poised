# S44b: answers

Asked by Claude in chat on 27 Oct 2026 (S43's open item "The anaesthetist's ASA grade cleared after Ready for admission"). Answered by Rahul the same day.

1. **Can a grade be changed or cleared?** **No, in any status.** Rahul: "I don't think we should let anyone change the grade once it has been graded already… it should be incremental… they can't change that previous one, but they can add another grading to it." Allowing changes is too risky.
2. **A reason:** **required for a re-grade** (every grade after the first); the first grade needs none.
3. **A grade entered by mistake:** **mark it "entered in error"**, with a reason. It stays visible, struck through, with who marked it, when and why; it no longer counts, and the one before it counts again.
