# S39 answers

Asked by the conductor in chat, 26 Oct 2026 (Rahul). Research behind the answers: [docs/research/c6-scores-asa.md](../../research/c6-scores-asa.md).

1. **Where a condition's usual ASA grade comes from.** Rahul asked for research ("spot on, by the book, as good as the mature products"). **Answer: a file built from the ASA's own published examples** (ASA Physical Status Classification, 2020 examples). Adding a condition fills in its grade with the example as the reason; **when the grade depends on severity** (e.g. diabetes: well controlled II, poorly controlled III), **the author picks which**. No silent single grade. The anaesthetist always gives the final grade ("the app suggests, a clinician confirms").
2. **The suggested ASA in conditions** (e.g. show an "Anaesthetic plan" clinician set only when it's III or more). **Answer: yes**, in conditions on clinician sets.
