# S15 build notes: patient accounts across trusts, sign-in links and the patient home

Builder, automatic run (`/run-sessions`), 13 Oct 2026. Rahul's answers are in [answers.md](answers.md):
1. **PAT-10 is built now** (it widens the brief, Rahul's choice): patients change their own mobile or email from the home, and a new contact is verified before it's used.
2. **Rate limits:** 3 sign-in links per mobile or email per 15 minutes; 10 per hour per IP address.

## What was built

### Data (migration `00013_patient_accounts.sql`; up, down, up checked on the local database)

- **`patient_accounts`:** `mobile` (E.164 or ''), `email` (lower case or ''), **`date_of_birth`** (the one proved when it was made), `created_at`. Indexed by mobile and by email. `patients.account_id` (added in S13) now references it, indexed.
- **`login_links`:** `patient_id` may be NULL; new `contact` and `account_id`; kinds `invite`, `signin` (a contact, expires) and **`verify`** (a new contact for an account, expires). One check constraint says which columns each kind needs.
- **`patient_sessions`:** `account_id` replaces `patient_id` (open sessions were ended by the migration: they last 30 minutes).
- **`rate_limit_hits`** (`key`, `at`): one row per message asked for; keys `contact:<mobile or email>` and `ip:<address>`. Rows older than a day go as new ones come; **`make seed` empties it**, so each test round starts uncounted.
- Indexes: the unjoined records by `(mobile, date_of_birth)` and `(email, date_of_birth)`; `episodes (patient_id, created_at DESC)` for the home.

### Go (`internal/httpapi/accounts.go`, unless said)

- **Accounts and joining (PAT-07, A-3).** After the right date of birth on an **invite**, `joinAccount` (one transaction, an advisory lock on the date of birth so two first visits can't make two accounts): the record joins the account with **the same date of birth and the same mobile or email** (the oldest if several), or a new account made from the record; a joined account takes a contact it lacks from the record (never replaces one). Then **the person's other unjoined records with that date of birth and one of those contacts join too** (any trust). After a **sign-in link**, the records with the typed contact and date of birth join.
- **The session is the account's**, so it opens the episodes of every record joined to it, in every trust (`loadPatientForm` checks `episode's record.account_id = session's account`). It's still tied to the link it was opened with: Sign out, a Resend or a lock on that link end it. A link's expiry governs only opening it, so a session from a 15-minute sign-in link lasts like any other (30 minutes idle).
- **`POST /api/p/signin {contact}`** (PAT-06): one field, a mobile (read as the deployment's `PHONE_COUNTRY` without a country code) or an email (`patient.Contact`). A mobile outside the country is **400** (PAT-11: "We can only send text messages to mobile numbers in this country. Please use your email address instead."). Then the **rate limits** (429, see below). A link goes only if an account signs in with that contact, but **the answer is always 202 `{channel}`**, so nobody learns who has an account (a provider failure is 502). Link `APP_URL/s/<token>`, valid **15 minutes**; text "Poised: your sign-in link. It works once, within 15 minutes: …"; email "Your Poised sign-in link".
- **`POST /api/p/signin/{token}/continue`** → `{}` (nothing about anyone; changes nothing). **`…/dob`**: the date of birth must be an account's among those with the link's contact (so two people sharing a phone each get their own home); right → the link is used up, matching records join, the session cookie, `{}`; wrong → "…You have N tries left." and **the fifth locks it** ("…ask for a new sign-in link"). **Used or expired → 404 `code: "expired"`**; unknown → 404. An invite token isn't a sign-in token, nor the other way round.
- **`GET /api/p/home`** (session): every episode of the account's records, **in every trust, newest first, in one query** (`ListHome`), with the hospital's name, questionnaire name, procedure, created date and a state (`to_do`, `in_progress` = any patient answers saved, `sent`). Suspended trusts and hospitals are left out (ORG-03). Plus `firstName` (the newest record's) and the account's **contacts in part** (`patient.MaskMobile` → `•••123`, `MaskEmail` → `s•••@example.org`). No date of birth, surname, identifiers or record ids.
- **`POST /api/p/contact {contact}`** (session; PAT-10): refuses the current contact, bad input and a mobile outside the country (400), then the same rate limits; revokes the account's earlier unused verification links of that kind; sends **`APP_URL/v/<token>`** (24 hours) to the **new** contact. Nothing changes yet.
- **`POST /api/p/contact/{token}/confirm`** (no session: the link proves the new contact, and may be opened on another device): sets the account's mobile or email **and every joined record's, in every trust**, uses the link up, and **tells the old contact** ("…was changed. If you didn't do this, please contact your hospital."). Refused with 409 if another account **born the same day** already signs in with that contact (sign-in would then be ambiguous). Used or expired → 404 `code: "expired"`.
- **Rate limits** (`allowMessage`, `hit`): before any sign-in or verification message, in one transaction with an advisory lock per key (always contact, then IP): 3 per contact per 15 minutes ("You've asked for 3 links in the last 15 minutes. Please use the newest one, or wait 15 minutes and try again."), 10 per IP address per hour ("There have been too many requests from this network. Please try again in an hour."). Refused requests aren't counted. The IP is `RemoteAddr`'s host (`clientIP`; see pending.md for S06).
- **Audit (S11):** `audited` can now write **several rows per request**, one per trust (and hospital) whose records it read or changed (`auditEntry.also`). `home.view` ("Patient opened their home"): a row per hospital listed, with its episodes and record. `account.join` ("Patient's record joined their sign-in account"): a row per trust whose record joined, written by the invite's or sign-in link's date-of-birth request. `contact.update` ("Patient changed their contact details"): a row per trust whose record changed. Not audited (no patient data read or changed, no trust): asking for a sign-in link, its Continue, a wrong date on it, and asking for a contact change.
- `patient.go`: the date-of-birth check is shared (`wrongDob`, `startSession`); the wrong-date message now says "our records" (it's also the sign-in link's). `requirePatient` puts the **account** in the context (`accountOf`).
- `internal/patient/contact.go`: `Contact` (mobile or email, PAT-11 check), `MaskMobile`, `MaskEmail`, `NotThisCountry`.
- Routes are in the **patient router** only (`no-store`, like the others).

### Web app

Why a link stopped is worked out by **`links.ts`** (`linkStopped`: locked, expired, unknown, suspended; a sign-in link's card has **Get a new sign-in link**). Tested in `links.test.ts`.

- **`RouteError.tsx`:** on a patient's page, "Go to the start page" becomes **Go to your questionnaires** (`/home`), never the staff sign-in (S04's pending item, `isPatientPage` in `links.ts`).
- `routes.tsx`: `/home`, `/signin`, `/s/:token`, `/v/:token` (pinned in `routes.test.ts`).

## Decided by the builder (automatic mode)

- **An account is a person: the date of birth is part of every match** (joining, sign-in). **Reason:** PAT-07 matches by "mobile or email" alone, but couples share an email and parents a phone; joining them would show one person another's questionnaires. With the date of birth they stay apart, and a shared contact's sign-in link lets whoever's date is entered see only their own (an automated test and T-13). PAT-08 (carers managing several people) is still to come.
- **The sign-in link belongs to the contact, not an account;** the date of birth picks the account. **Reason:** one message to a shared phone, not one per person.
- **Joining also takes the person's other unjoined records with the same date of birth and a contact that just reached them**, at an invite's or a sign-in link's date of birth. **Reason:** otherwise Trust B's questionnaire wouldn't appear on the home until the patient opened Trust B's own invite, which defeats "one home lists both" (brief, manual test 1). The proof is the same as an invite's: a link reached them at that contact and they knew the date of birth.
- **Sign-in is for accounts only:** a contact known only from a record never opened gets nothing (the same screen). **Reason:** PAT-06 is the returning patient's; a first-timer's invite works until the episode closes. The screen says so.
- **The patient's contact change is made to every trust's record** (not only the account), so invites and reminders reach them. **Reason:** S13's answer kept silent links from editing records and named PAT-10 as the deliberate way; it's audited per trust. No General notes entry is written on the episodes.
- **The old contact is told** after a change. **Reason:** a session left open on a shared phone could otherwise move the account to someone else's contact unnoticed.
- **Contacts on the home are shown in part** (`•••123`, `s•••@example.org`). **Reason:** S14's rule (after the check, no identifiers the patient didn't type); enough to recognise them.
- **Verification links last 24 hours** (sign-in links 15 minutes, as PAT-05). A newer request replaces the older link of the same kind.
- **One box for "mobile number or email address"** on `/signin` (the server tells which). **Reason:** simplest for older patients.
- **The home's "open episodes" are all of them** (no status closes an episode until C3), including sent ones (shown as **Sent**).
- **PX-08 is partly built:** hospital, questionnaire, procedure, state. The logo, files, videos and contact details are later plans'.
- **The IP limit counts every message request from one address**, sign-in and contact change alike, and refused requests don't count.

## Differences from the plan

- C2 lists `POST /api/p/signin` with "mobile or email"; it's `{contact}` (one field). The sign-in link's routes are `/api/p/signin/{token}/continue|dob` (the invite's stay `/api/p/links/…`). PAT-10 adds `POST /api/p/contact` and `POST /api/p/contact/{token}/confirm`, and the page `/v/:token`.
- `login_links` gained `verify` links, `contact` and `account_id`; `patient_sessions` hold an account, not a patient record.
- `patient_accounts` holds the date of birth (the plan had mobile and email only), for the reason above.

## For the pending list

- **S06 must make `clientIP` the real client's address** behind API Gateway / Amplify's rewrite (today `RemoteAddr`): otherwise every patient shares one address, and 10 sign-ins an hour would be the whole country's.
- **S14's X-1 is partly better:** leaving the form by **← Your questionnaires** or the browser's Back to the home is an in-app route change, so a change waiting for its autosave is sent. A reload or closing the tab within ~0.6 s still loses it (not widened, as asked).

- A patient can change or add a contact, not remove one.
- Joins and wrong dates of birth on sign-in links are in no trust's log when no record was touched (no trust); a security log may want them, like S14's probe item.
