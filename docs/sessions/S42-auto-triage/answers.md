# S42 answers

Asked by the conductor in chat, 26 Oct 2026 (Rahul). Research behind the answers: [docs/research/c6-scores-asa.md](../../research/c6-scores-asa.md).

1. **Leaving the Auto-triaged list:** a clinician clicks **Checked** (name and time recorded).
2. **What auto-triage does** (Rahul: "auto-triage should go straight to ready and the comms go out; configurable when the HQ is created"; research done). **Answer: a setting on each HQ; by default an auto-triaged episode moves straight to Ready and the patient's messages go out at once.** Safety checks always apply and can't be switched off: the server check ran, no score is Incomplete, no amber or red flags, and the rules pass their test cases. The episode also shows on the Auto-triaged list for the Checked step. The message wording avoids "fit for surgery". **Before go-live (C10):** the trust's clinical safety officer signs off this default (NHS England expects supervised triage).
