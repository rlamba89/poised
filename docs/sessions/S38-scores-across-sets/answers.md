# S38 answers

Asked by the conductor in chat, 26 Oct 2026 (Rahul). Research behind the answers: [docs/research/c6-scores-asa.md](../../research/c6-scores-asa.md).

1. **Points from age, sex, BMI and earlier answers.** Rahul first asked for research ("configurable, automatic, not rigid; formula-based is fine but clinicians never write formulas; by the book"). **Answer: menu lines**: "Add N points when [Patient › Age] [is over] [50]", from menus (Patient, Measurements, Answers in other sets, Other scores). **A score with a missing input shows "Incomplete" and is never treated as low risk.**
2. **Ready-made scores.** Rahul first said "later, in C7", then, after the research, chose **STOP-Bang now**: a locked, by-the-book STOP-Bang in C6 (published wording, points and bands; its published examples as test cases; authors can't change it). Other ready-made scores (Apfel and the rest) stay in C7. **Before go-live:** STOP-Bang's commercial use needs a licence from University Health Network (Toronto): record it for C10.
